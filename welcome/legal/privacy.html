Privacy Policy

Effective [launch date]

The short version
  • Everything you make in Sedi stays in your browser, on your device. There's no Sedi server and no account, so I can't see it.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • A few optional features talk to other services, only when you use them. They're all listed below.
  • If you buy a membership, Stripe handles the payment and I receive your name and email so I can deliver what you paid for.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, goals, calendar, notes, links, files and settings are stored in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on the device you're using. They're never sent to me. That also means:

  • Only you can back them up. Use Menu → Download data to save a copy.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or switching browser or device means starting fresh unless you import a backup.
  • Keys you add for other AI services are stored only on your device and are never included in exports.

What leaves your device, and when

Sedi only contacts another service when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Weather (only if you add it): the city you search for, then your chosen location's coordinates, are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. Nothing is sent until you choose.
  • Saved links: to show a link's icon, your browser asks that website for its icon, much as visiting it would.
  • On-device AI and voice (if you turn them on): the models are downloaded once from public servers (jsDelivr and Hugging Face). After that they run on your device, and your notes and voice never leave it.
  • Other AI services (if you add your own key): what you ask, plus the notes needed to answer, are sent directly from your browser to the provider you chose (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) under that provider's terms and privacy policy.
  • Voice typing in the browser: until the private on-device voice model is ready, some browsers send dictated audio to their own speech service (for example, Chrome sends it to Google) to turn it into text.
  • Google: “Open as Google Doc” and “Add to Google Calendar” open Google's own pages with the note or event you chose. If you connect a Google account (optional), Sedi asks Google only for access to the documents it creates for you, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Apple Reminders: handled by the Shortcuts app on your own device.

The website itself

Sedi and this site are served by a hosting provider ([GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages]), which, like any web host, may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the service secure and running. The site doesn't load fonts, scripts or images from anyone else. The free guides are hosted with the site and downloaded directly to your device.

If you buy a membership

Payments are processed by Stripe. I never see or store your full card number. I receive your name, email address, country and purchase details. I use them only to:

  • confirm your purchase and send receipts,
  • deliver the Mac and iPhone apps and other membership benefits, and tell you when they're ready,
  • handle refunds and support, and keep the records tax law requires.

I don't sell or share your information, and I won't send you marketing unless you choose to sign up for it. I keep purchase records for as long as Canadian tax rules require (generally six years) and delete what I no longer need.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you (in practice, purchase and support emails) by writing to hello@[your-domain]. I'll respond within 30 days. Data inside Sedi is already fully in your control: download it, or erase it with Menu → Clear storage.

Children

Sedi isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes
