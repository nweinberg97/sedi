Help

Quick answers, and how to reach me.

Inside Sedi, tap the ? button in the top right for step-by-step help with any tool. It works offline.

Getting started

How do I start?

Open Sedi at this address. There's nothing to sign up for. A few example cards show you the gestures; delete them whenever you like.

How do I keep Sedi handy so I don't lose it?

Install it like an app. iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Mac: in Safari, choose File → Add to Dock. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar. At the very least, bookmark it. On Apple devices, installing also stops Safari from clearing Sedi's data when you haven't opened it for a while.

What are Taskly, Boardly, Timely and Brainly?

Taskly is your to-do board. Boardly holds life goals by area. Timely is your calendar, routines, projects and goals. Brainly keeps notes, folders and links. The Universal Board (the planet button) is an inbox for anything you haven't sorted yet, and the atom is the assistant: tap to type, hold to talk.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, on your device. There's no Sedi server and no account. Open Menu → Your data in Sedi to see how much is stored and when you last backed up.

How do I back up, or move to another device?

In Sedi, choose Menu → Download data to save one file. On the other device or browser, choose Menu → Import data and pick that file. Sedi reminds you to back up once a week.

What can erase my data?

Clearing “cookies and site data” for Sedi (in Safari, “history and website data”), using a private window, or uninstalling the browser. Clearing only cached images and files doesn't touch it. Keep a backup.

Features

Does Sedi work offline?

Yes. After your first visit it opens and runs without a connection. Only weather and services you connect need the internet.

How do voice and AI work?

Tap the atom to type, or hold it to talk. Sedi answers from your own notes. You can download a small private AI model that runs entirely on your device (Menu → On-device AI), or connect your own AI provider (Menu → Other AI services).

Can I add things to my calendar or reminders?

Yes. Events can go to Google Calendar or any calendar app as an .ics file, notes can open as Google Docs, and tasks can go to Apple Reminders through a one-time Shortcuts setup (Menu → Apple Reminders).
