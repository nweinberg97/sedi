The name

Order, and a place to sit with it.

Sedi comes from two old roots. Together they describe what the app is for.

Seder סֵדֶרHebrew

Order. Things arranged in a meaningful sequence, each one in its proper place.

SedēreLatin

To sit. To settle, and to take your place. The same root gives us seat and session.

First, everything finds its place. Then you can sit down with your life and see it clearly: what matters, what you’re working toward and what needs you today.

Principles

How Sedi is made.

Less is more.

Every screen does one job. Empty space is part of the design, and nothing is added just because it could be.

Limits on purpose.

Taskly’s columns don’t scroll and Boardly stops at ten boards. When something fills up, you decide what matters instead of piling it up.

Value before sign-up.

Click a link and you’re in. No account, no email, no card, and no countdown to a trial ending.

Your data stays home.

Everything is stored in your browser on your own computer. Download all of it as a file whenever you like.

Made for focus.

The desk is where deep work happens. Sedi is designed for a real screen and a quiet hour, not for notifications.

Works with what you use.

Sedi sends things onward to your calendar, Google Docs and Apple Reminders. It’s a center, not a wall around your tools.

Private by design

A quiet room that belongs to you.

Sedi runs in your browser and keeps working with no connection after your first visit. Your cards live on your computer, not on someone else’s server.

Questions

Plain answers.

Is Sedi really free?

Yes. The web app is free forever, with no time limit and no credit card. Lifetime Membership is for people who want Sedi on their Mac and iPhone, synced.

Does Sedi replace my notes app or calendar?

No. Sedi is where you think and decide in focus. It works with the tools you already use: it adds events to your calendar, opens notes in Google Docs and sends tasks to Apple Reminders.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s storage, on your own computer. Nothing is sent to a server. Menu → Download data saves everything as a file, and Import data brings it back.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the link and start.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, Sedi opens and runs with no connection. Only weather and services you connect yourself need the internet.

Is there a Mac or iPhone app?

They’re being built now, and they’ll stay connected to Sedi on the web. Lifetime members get both.

Why do the columns stop scrolling?

Limits make you choose. When a column is full, you finish, move or let go of something instead of letting a list grow forever.

What does the AI do?

The assistant searches and summarizes your own cards, and can create or move them after you confirm. Sovereign Brain is an optional coach that runs on your computer. You can also connect your own AI provider in settings.
