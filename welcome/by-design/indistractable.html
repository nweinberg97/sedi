The big idea

Distraction is an escape from discomfort

Eyal argues that most distraction is an attempt to escape an uncomfortable feeling such as boredom, loneliness or worry. Technology is only the proximate cause. Learn to handle the feeling, plan your time around your values, and the pull of distraction loses much of its power.3,4

Three lessons

1

Traction vs. distraction

Traction is any action that moves you toward what you intend; distraction moves you away. You can only call something a distraction if you know what you meant to do.3

2

Ask of every trigger

Is this trigger serving me, or am I serving it? Keep the pings that lead to traction and remove the rest.3,6

3

Willpower isn't a battery

Eyal reviews research suggesting that believing willpower runs out makes us give up sooner. Reimagine your temperament as well as the task.4

The framework

The Indistractable Model3,4

Eyal's four-part model, from the inside out.3

1

Master internal triggers

Deal with the discomfort

Notice the feeling that comes before distraction, and stay with it instead of escaping.3

Try: The 10-minute rule: wait ten minutes before giving in.3,5
2

Make time for traction

Plan your values into time

Timebox your week across three domains: You, Relationships and Work.3

Try: Schedule sync: agree your calendar with those who depend on it.4,6
3

Hack back external triggers

Pings, dings and interruptions

Tactics for email, group chat, meetings, your phone and your desktop.3,4

Try: No agenda, no meeting.6
4

Prevent distraction with pacts

Decide in advance

Effort pacts add friction, price pacts put money at stake, and identity pacts change how you see yourself.3

Try: Call yourself indistractable, and act like it.3

From the book

Tools to try

The Distraction Tracker

Eyal's log for finding your internal triggers.3

  1. Keep a card or note open while you work.
  2. Each time you get distracted, write the time and the feeling just before.
  3. After three days, look for the most common feelings and triggers.
  4. Choose one fix for the biggest one.

The timeboxed week

Eyal's way to turn values into a calendar.3

  1. Start from a blank weekly template.
  2. Add blocks for You, then Relationships, then Work.
  3. Follow it for a week, then reflect and refine.
  4. Share it with your manager or partner.

Make it yours

Try it this week

  1. Run the Distraction Tracker for three days.
  2. Try the 10-minute rule on the most common urge.
  3. Turn off every notification that doesn't come from a person.
  4. Timebox next week across You, Relationships and Work.
